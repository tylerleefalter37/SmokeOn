SQUADRON STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP)

Comprehensive breakdown of all simulation systems, flight physics envelopes, DACT tactics, and logistics.

01. AIR SHOW WEATHER ENVELOPES & MINIMUMS (FAA ORDER 8900.1)

High Show Profile (Ceiling ≥ 8,000 ft, Visibility ≥ 3 SM): Full aerobatic clearance granted. Authorized for high-altitude vertical climbs, Bomb Bursts with 6-way downward breaks, Fleur-de-Lis maneuvers, Delta Loops, and Vertical Defensive Spirals.

Low Show Profile (Ceiling 3,500 – 8,000 ft): Vertical maneuvers are prohibited. Vertical loops and breaks are dynamically converted into horizontal rolling scissors, cloverleafs, Delta Rolls, and high-G knife-edge passes.

Flat Show Profile (Ceiling 1,500 – 3,500 ft): High-altitude and inverted maneuvers prohibited. Flight line executes low passes, echelon flybys, and high-speed sneak passes strictly level along the runway axis.

Show Box Safety Line (1,500-Ft Buffer): Cat III tactical jet demonstrations must maintain 1,500 ft of lateral separation from spectator fence lines. Any pilot pointing a lift vector directly into the spectator box must execute an immediate break away.

02. FLIGHT DEMO PILOT SCREENING ("THE RUSH") & 3-YEAR TOURS

Every winter, candidates from combat fighter wings submit dossiers to join the demonstration team. Demonstration pilots serve a maximum 3-year tour of duty before rotating back to combat fleets, requiring ongoing recruitment:

  • Phase 1 (Paper Screening): Verifies minimum 1,250 tactical carrier/strike fighter hours, combat medals, and impeccable flight evaluation reports.
  • Phase 2 (Social Rush Dinner): Evaluates team chemistry, personal ego control, and psychological poise under high social stress. The entire team must vote unanimously.
  • Phase 3 (Backseat Hop-Ride): Flown in the two-seat Jet #7. The candidate is subjected to 7.5+ Gs, rapid rolls, and close-proximity formation tracking to evaluate raw stick-and-rudder reflexes.
  • 3-Year Mandatory Tour Rotation: Demonstration duty is limited to 3 continuous seasons. At the end of every season, pilots finishing Year 3 retire, requiring rush board vetting to fill vacancies during winter workups.

03. FLEET MAINTENANCE, ENGINE REPLACEMENTS & SPARE SWAPPING

Mission Capability Statuses:

  • FMC (Fully Mission Capable): Airframe is cleared for all aerobatic and high-G combat maneuvers.
  • PMC (Partially Mission Capable): Minor hydraulic or smoke pod defects. Usable for transit or light practice, but degrades show performance.
  • NMC (Non-Mission Capable / Grounded): Engine core wear, spar crack, or severe structural fatigue. Aircraft is forbidden from flying until overhauled.

Jet #8 Spare Swapping: If an active flight-line jet (#1 through #6) suffers mechanical failure, Jet #8 can be hot-swapped into the slot immediately from the depot bay.

Turbine Core Life: High-G afterburner runs deplete engine life. When turbine core drops below 50%, procurement of a new factory core is required.

04. DISSIMILAR AIR COMBAT (DACT) & ROUTINE CHOREOGRAPHY

Unlike standard uniform flight teams, this squadron features mixed-fleet capability. Pairing dissimilar opposing aircraft unlocks significant crowd excitement bonuses:

  • Korean War Dogfight Preset: F-86 Sabre vs Mikoyan MiG-15. Transonic horizontal scissors and vertical zooms simulating MiG Alley (+15% crowd excitement).
  • Red Flag Aggressor Preset: Mikoyan MiG-29 vs F-16C Viper / F-22A Raptor. Post-stall pitchbacks, simulated flare dumps, and tactical merges (+15% crowd excitement).
  • Aggressiveness Compounding Risk: Each maneuver increases routine crowd excitement, but adds physical fatigue and risk percentage. Long 10+ maneuver routines in high heat exponentially increase emergency probability.

05. IN-FLIGHT EMERGENCIES, EJECTION & INVESTIGATION BOARDS

Air shows carry inherent risk. In-flight abnormalities require rapid tactical decisions:

  • G-LOC Tunnel Vision: High sustained G-loads drain cranial blood pressure. Pilots must ease back on the stick or face total loss of consciousness.
  • Bird Strikes & Compressor Stalls: Low-level merges risk bird ingestion. Pilots can attempt airstart restarts or establish zero-zero ejection attitude away from populated areas.
  • Catastrophic Mishaps: An unrecoverable crash causes total hull loss, pilot moral collapse, and a safety investigation board that triples annual hull liability insurance.

06. SPONSORSHIP PORTFOLIO & SQUADRON FINANCIAL LEDGER

15 commercial and aerospace sponsors are divided across three operational tiers:

  • Grassroots (Low-Range): Local supply stores and fueling stations. Low prestige thresholds, immediate recurring cash flow ($15k–$28k/wk).
  • Commercial Aviation (Mid-Range): Avionics and chronometer manufacturers. Requires moderate prestige (65+) and clean safety records ($38k–$55k/wk + per-show bonuses).
  • Aerospace Giants (High-Range): Defense contractors and orbital launch foundations. Demands elite prestige (80+), high routine excitement, and zero crash history ($75k–$135k/wk + $50k+ per-show bonuses).
