STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP)

DEMONSTRATION PILOT'S NATOPS / AFMAN CHECKLIST

FAA Order 8900.1 / USAF 11-2F-16 Vol 3 / USN OPNAV 3710 Demonstration Rules

1. WEATHER PROFILES (HIGH, LOW, FLAT)

High Show: Ceiling ≥ 8,000 ft, visibility ≥ 3 nm. Authorizes full vertical dimension (Bomb Burst w/ vertical crosses, Fleur-de-Lis, High Alpha loops).

Low Show: Ceiling 3,500 ft to 8,000 ft. Pure vertical loops eliminated. Rolling maneuvers (Delta Roll, Cloverleaf, Minimum Radius Turns) remain authorized.

Flat Show: Ceiling 1,500 ft to 3,500 ft. Zero vertical or inverted maneuvers permitted over show center. Strictly horizontal flybys, echelon passes, and sneak passes.

2. CROWD LINE & FAA 500-FT BOX

Aerobatic Box: Cat III jet demonstrations must maintain at least 1,500 feet of lateral separation from the primary spectator fence at all times.

Velocity Vector Rule: No aircraft energy vector may point directly toward spectators while in maneuvering flight.

"KNOCK IT OFF" Call: If any pilot experiences spatial disorientation, G-LOC signs, bird strike, or engine stall, ANY member can call "Knock It Off!" The entire demonstration pauses immediately.

3. MAINTENANCE READINESS GRADES

FMC (Fully Mission Capable): Systems 100% operational, engine core clean, smoke injector calibrated.

PMC (Partially Mission Capable): Minor snag (smoke pod pressure drop, UHF aux radio static). Allowed to fly non-solo roles or spare position.

NMC (Non-Mission Capable): Grounded. Hydraulic leak, turbine blade crack, or severe airframe stress. Must undergo depot repair or full engine replacement.
